What this is. A 29-week plan built around three sessions a week: a Tuesday evening track session, an optional solo easy run mid-week, and a long group run on the weekend. Three pace groups targeting 1:50, 2:00, and 2:10 finish times — use the filter tabs to show your group's paces.

How it's structured. Standard periodization: base (wks 1–8), build (wks 9–18), race-specific (wks 19–26), taper (wks 27–29). Progression comes from shorter recovery and higher intensity — not more laps. Track sessions stay at ~5km active throughout. Cutback weeks land every 3–4 weeks to let the body adapt before loading again.

Singapore-specific. Evening heat adds ~20 sec/km to effort — run by feel, not GPS. Long runs no later than 6am, ideally 5:30am. December is cooler (NE monsoon); race conditions will be the best you train in all year. The 4:30am flag-off helps too.
